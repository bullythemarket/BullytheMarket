💧 How the liquidity pool works

The liquidity pool holds both BNB and $BTM. When someone buys, they send BNB in and receive $BTM out — when someone sells, the reverse. The pool is what makes trading possible without needing another person on the other side of every trade.

Pool depth directly determines trade size an investor can make without significant price impact. Growing this pool — through presale proceeds, trading fees, and eventually community-provided liquidity via farming — is an ongoing priority, not a one-time event.
